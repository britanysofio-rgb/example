ToxCase

Randomized toxicology cases with a pharmacotherapy focus — for teaching and self-study

In Unknown ingestion mode the history withholds the substance and confirmatory drug levels — the learner recognizes the toxidrome and treats empirically, then names the toxin. Reveal the answer to check. (Keep category on "Any" so the case stays blind.)

Click Generate new case to create a unique patient encounter.

Every case randomizes the patient, dose, timing, vitals, and labs — consistent with the toxidrome — and computes weight-based antidote dosing for the generated patient.

For education only. Cases are simulated. Doses shown are common teaching references — always verify against current institutional protocols and a poison center (in the US: 1-800-222-1222) before applying to real patients.
